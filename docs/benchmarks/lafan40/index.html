02 · Evaluation setup

SettingSpecification
RobotUnitree G1 · 29 actuated joints · canonical benchmark asset
EngineMuJoCo 3.17.0 · 2 ms physics step
InitializationFirst reference pose and canonical finite-difference velocities; histories reset independently per clip.
EnvironmentNominal: no observation noise, no domain randomization; sliding friction 1.
ControllerAuthor-native control frequency, observations, history, normalization, gains and action processing.
ScoringEvery 0.02 s after physics, plus the exact reference endpoint.
State accessIdeal-proprioceptive-no-position-v1: joint state, full orientation and ideal root velocities; no actual position, contacts or explicit odometry.
  • Scored body origins (14): pelvis, left_hip_yaw_link, right_hip_yaw_link, torso_link, left_knee_link, right_knee_link, left_ankle_roll_link, right_ankle_roll_link, left_shoulder_yaw_link, right_shoulder_yaw_link, left_elbow_link, right_elbow_link, left_wrist_yaw_link, right_wrist_yaw_link.
