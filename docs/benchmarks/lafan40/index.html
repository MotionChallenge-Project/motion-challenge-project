03 · Success & failure

A motion passes only if it reaches the exact final reference timestamp without any condition below firing. All comparisons use the actual and reference states at the same time.

ConditionMeasurementFailure threshold
Torso heightAbsolute world-Z error of torso_link.> 0.25 m
Torso tiltDifference in the torso’s inclination to gravity: compare the local Z component of unit gravity in the actual and reference torso frames.> 0.8 · dimensionless
Wrist / ankle heightMaximum absolute world-Z error across the four designated end effectors.> 0.25 m
  • Any condition is sufficient. Threshold equality does not fail; a failure at the final timestamp still counts.
  • Height is relative to the reference, not a minimum standing height. Lying down or touching the floor is not automatically a failure.
  • With local-to-world torso rotations R_actual and R_ref and unit gravity g = (0, 0, −1), tilt error is |(R_actualᵀg)_z − (R_refᵀg)_z|. It measures inclination to gravity, not yaw or the full relative rotation. Equal inclination can produce zero error even with different leaning directions.
  • End effectors: left_ankle_roll_link, right_ankle_roll_link, left_wrist_yaw_link, right_wrist_yaw_link.
  • No XY or yaw alignment is applied to success checks. The three alignments are used only for reporting tracking errors; they do not change success or completion.

04 · Tracking-error coverage

Tracking errors are reported over two time ranges. Success and completion always use the first failure; recovering later does not turn a failed motion into a success.

Time rangeFrames included
Early terminationFrom the first frame through the first failing sample, including that sample. If no failure occurs, use the entire motion.
No terminationAll evaluation samples through the exact final reference timestamp, including states after failure. The robot is not reset after a failure.
  • Both ranges use the same three alignment definitions below. Read tracking errors together with success and completion.

05 · Metrics

MPBPE is reported for each time range under three alignment settings, giving six tracking-error measurements.

MetricDefinitionBetter
SuccessNumber of complete motions without failure, divided by all 40 motions.Higher
CompletionLast valid sample time divided by full reference duration, averaged equally across motions.Higher
MPBPE · mmMean Euclidean position error of 14 named body origins. Average bodies and samples within each motion, then average motions equally.Lower
  • XY + yaw: remove pelvis XY translation and the closest relative world-Z rotation. Preserve absolute height and tilt.
  • XY only: remove pelvis XY translation. Preserve heading, absolute height and tilt.
  • No alignment: compare world-frame positions directly, retaining translation and heading drift.
  • The same 14 metric bodies are used in every view: pelvis, left_hip_yaw_link, right_hip_yaw_link, torso_link, left_knee_link, right_knee_link, left_ankle_roll_link, right_ankle_roll_link, left_shoulder_yaw_link, right_shoulder_yaw_link, left_elbow_link, right_elbow_link, left_wrist_yaw_link, right_wrist_yaw_link.
  • Degenerate yaw alignment is flagged and retained. Tracking accuracy should be read together with success and coverage.
